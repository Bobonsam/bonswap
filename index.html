How it works. This page talks to the Ammora protocol contracts on GIWA Sepolia (chain 91342) through the public SDK. Quotes come from ALMMClient.quoteExactInput; swaps are planned by AmmoraSwapClient.prepareExactIn (model almm, direct-wallet execution) and signed in your wallet — approvals, minimum output and deadline are shown before you confirm. Testnet pools are canary markets, so liquidity is thin and some quotes fill only partially.
